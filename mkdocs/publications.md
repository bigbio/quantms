## Citing Tools Used by quantms

quantms integrates several tools. Please cite the relevant ones based on what your run used.

> **Comet: an open-source MS/MS sequence database search tool.**
> *Proteomics.* 2013;13(1):22–24.
> [DOI: 10.1002/pmic.201200439](https://doi.org/10.1002/pmic.201200439)

**MS-GF+**
> Kim S, Pevzner PA.
> **MS-GF+ makes progress towards a universal database search tool for proteomics.**
> *Nature Communications.* 2014;5:5277.
> [DOI: 10.1038/ncomms6277](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms6277)

**Sage**
> Lazear MR.

**Percolator**

**MS2Rescore**
> Declercq A, Bouwmeester R, Chiva C, et al.
